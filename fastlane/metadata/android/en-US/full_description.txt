Biking is a modern, privacy-friendly bike tracking app for Android.

It combines GPS with the phone’s motion sensors (rotation vector + linear acceleration) and an adaptive Kalman filter to deliver stable and responsive speed readings — even when the phone is in a pocket or mounted at an angle.

Features:
• Hybrid speed tracking (GPS + sensors)
• Smart moving/stopped detection and auto-pause
• Live map with route polyline and follow mode
• Local ride history
• Elevation gain/loss
• GPX export
• Foreground service with live notification
• Unit switch (km/h ↔ mph) and theme support

All ride data stays only on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no cloud.
